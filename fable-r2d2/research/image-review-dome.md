# Image review: dome and head

Category: dome and head. Reviewed 2026-09-12 for the fable-r2d2 package.
Focus: dome panel layout (pie panels, top disc, side panels), radar eye housing, the
three holoprojectors, front logic displays, rear logic display, PSI positions, blue/silver
pattern, dome rotation ring, and how the dome sits on the body ring.

Every file below is stored in `C:/dev/robots/fable-r2d2/research/images/`. Every file was
downloaded with curl, checked with Pillow (all 19 open as JPEG, all larger than 90 kB, no
duplicate MD5), and opened and viewed with the Read tool before it was described.

Naming used below follows the Printed Droid terminology page
(https://www.printed-droid.com/kb/r2-d2-terminology/), read on 2026-09-12: pie panels
PP1-PP6, lower dome panels P1-P14, holoprojectors HP1 (front), HP2 (rear), HP3 (top), front
logic displays (FLDs, two windows), rear logic display (RLD, one window), front PSI, rear PSI,
magic panel (P5), dome buttons H1 and H2, dome ring. That page gives no dimensions.

Numbers below are one of two kinds:
- "read from source": the number is quoted from the named URL.
- "estimated": the number was measured by eye from pixel positions in the named image and
  is given as a ratio of the dome diameter D. D = 463.55 mm at club scale and 317.0 mm at
  this project's scale (both read from `C:/dev/robots/fable-r2d2/plan.md`, Locked decisions).
  Ratios measured on a curved surface in a photograph are approximate (about +/- 10 %).

## Key findings for CAD

1. Dome geometry: model the dome as a slightly tall ovoid, not a true hemisphere. Height from
   the top of the dome ring to the crown is about 0.60-0.62 D (estimated from dome-01, dome-10,
   dome-16). At D = 317.0 mm that is about 190-197 mm; with the ring (0.09 D, about 29 mm) the
   whole head is about 220-225 mm tall, which fits the 320 mm print height.
2. Dome ring: a separate-looking silver band about 0.09 D tall (about 29 mm at project scale)
   with a thin blue band of about 0.02 D (about 6 mm) directly above it. The ring outer diameter
   equals the body outer diameter; the ring overlaps the top of the body by about 0.05 D
   (dome-07, dome-15, dome-18). In the one-piece dome STL, print the ring as a short vertical
   cylinder skirt with a rebate on the inside for the lazy-susan bearing and the friction-drive
   wheel contact face (dome-09 and dome-11 show a flat inner annular plate about 0.08-0.10 D
   wide at the rim that carries electronics; reproduce it as an inner ledge).
3. Three-zone blue/silver pattern: (a) lower band of blue panels P1-P14 about 0.20-0.22 D
   tall starting directly above the blue band; (b) a plain silver zone; (c) six 60-degree blue
