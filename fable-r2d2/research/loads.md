Written 2026-09-12. This is an engineering screening, not a certification. Every number is
tagged **[S]** (measured from a source; the URL is given) or **[A]** (assumed or estimated by
this note). Geometry is the pre-CAD basis given in `plan.md`; re-run the arithmetic when
`cad/params.scad` fixes the real dimensions. Every formula is written out in its section so
the numbers can be regenerated by hand or script.

## 1. Mass budget

Method: printed parts are taken as surface area x wall thickness x fill fraction x 1.27 g/cm³.
A 4 mm wall printed with 5 perimeters of 0.42 mm per side is solid (5 x 0.42 x 2 = 4.2 mm >
4 mm), so thick walls use fill fraction 0.92 [A]. Decks printed with 2 walls plus 30 %
gyroid use effective fraction (2 x 0.84 + 0.30 x 3.32) / 5 = 0.54 [A]. Body-panel cutouts
(doors, vents, shoulder holes) remove 12 % of the shell [A].

### Body (two PETG rings, 317 mm diameter, 385 mm tall, 4 mm wall)

- Shell area = π x 317 x 385 = 383,416 mm². Volume at 4 mm = 1,534 cm³. Solid mass = 1,948 g.
  Net = 1,534 x 0.92 x 0.88 x 1.27 = **1,577 g** [A].
- Four ring ribs 8 x 4 mm at Ø309: annulus π/4 (309² - 293²) x 4 mm = 30.3 cm³ each. Six
  vertical ribs 385 x 8 x 3 mm = 9.2 cm³ each. (4 x 30.3 + 6 x 9.2) x 1.27 = **224 g** [A].
- Bottom deck Ø317 x 5 mm at 0.54: 395 cm³ x 0.54 x 1.27 = **268 g** [A]. Battery cradle
  walls **120 g** [A].
- Electronics tray 250 x 200 x 4 mm at 0.54 plus standoffs: **166 g** [A].
- Two shoulder bosses Ø80 x 30 mm at 0.7: 151 cm³ x 0.7 x 1.27 = 134 g each, **268 g** [A].
- Head ring (dome bearing seat) annulus Ø317 to Ø257 x 6 mm at 0.7: **144 g** [A].
- Surface details (skirt, coin slots, vents, door panels, utility-arm blanks): **250 g** [A].
- Body printed total: 1,577 + 224 + 268 + 120 + 166 + 268 + 144 + 250 = **3,018 g**
  (range 2,600-3,400 g).

### Dome (one piece, 317 mm diameter, 186 mm tall, 3 mm wall)

- Hemisphere 2π x 158.5² = 157,860 mm² plus a 27.5 mm cylindrical skirt π x 317 x 27.5 =
  27,390 mm². Total 185,235 mm² x 3 mm = 556 cm³. A 3 mm wall is solid at 5 perimeters, so
  shell = 556 x 1.27 = 706 g. Panel detail and holoprojector bosses +20 %, lip ring +100 g:
  **947 g** (range 800-1,100 g) [A].

### Head drive

Motor 30.6 g [S] + wheel 38 g [S] + pivoting mount and tension screw 100 g [A] + lazy susan
ring ~250 g [A; a 12 in steel unit is heavier, a 10 in aluminium unit lighter; not weighed] +
slip ring ~20 g [A] = **440 g** (range 350-600 g).

### Outer legs (two, 430 mm shoulder to ankle, two prints each, two M8 rods each)

- Leg box section 80 x 50 mm outside, 4 mm walls [A]: wall area 80 x 50 - 72 x 42 = 976 mm²
  x 430 mm = 420 cm³ at 0.92.
- Shoulder hub disc Ø127 x 35 mm at 0.45 = 443 cm³ x 0.45; ankle clevis 100 cm³ at 0.7; rod
  bosses and ribs 80 cm³ at 0.9.
- Printed = (378 + 199 + 70 + 72) x 1.27 = **913 g** per leg [A].
- Two M8 threaded rods 430 mm: effective diameter 7.2 mm [A] gives 40.7 mm² x 430 mm x
  7.85 g/cm³ = 137 g each, 275 g per leg. Nuts and washers 40 g.
- Per leg **1,228 g**; two legs **2,457 g** (range 2,100-2,800 g).

### Centre leg with caster ankle

Print 400 g + caster pivot bolt, two 608 bearings, spacers 100 g = **500 g** (range 400-600 g) [A].

### Feet (three)

Result: the arithmetic gives **13.3 kg nominal, range 11.6 to 15.0 kg**. This is above the
8-11 kg expectation. The cause is the 4 mm body wall: a solid 4 mm PETG skin on a 317 x 385
mm cylinder alone weighs 1.95 kg before ribs and decks. To reach 11 kg [A]: 3 mm body walls
with the same ribs (-0.4 kg), 3 mm foot walls (-0.35 kg), 15 % infill decks (-0.15 kg),
lightened shoulder hubs (-0.2 kg), 2 x 6 mm body rods in place of 4 x M8 (-0.3 kg), and a
0.9 kg budget cap on the dome. The rest of this note carries the **13.3 kg nominal (W =
130.2 N)** and shows the 11 kg case where it changes a conclusion.

## 2. Traction and drive

Wheel radius r = 0.0315 m [S, 63 mm diameter].

- Stall torque T_stall = 0.8 kgf·cm = 0.8 x 9.80665 x 0.01 = **0.0785 N·m** [S at 6 V].
- Force per motor at stall F = T/r = 0.0785 / 0.0315 = **2.49 N**. Six motors: **14.94 N**.
- Torque constant, assuming torque rises linearly with current above the no-load current
  [A]: k_t = 0.0785 / (1.5 - 0.15) = **0.0581 N·m/A**.
- At the DRV8833 1 A limit [S]: T = 0.0581 x (1.0 - 0.15) = 0.0494 N·m, F = **1.57 N** per
  motor, six motors **9.41 N**. Motor speed at that load = 200 x (1 - 0.0494/0.0785) =
  **74 RPM** = 0.24 m/s. Mechanical output = 0.0494 x 7.75 rad/s = 0.38 W of 6 W input; the
  other 5.6 W is heat. Treat 1 A as a short-burst limit, not a continuous rating [A]. At an
  assumed thermally safe 0.6 A continuous [A]: T = 0.0262 N·m, F = 0.83 N per motor, 4.98 N
  for six.
- Rolling resistance F_rr = Crr x W [S formula]. Small solid silicone wheels on a hard floor are
  taken at Crr 0.02 and 0.04 [A; car tyres on concrete are 0.010-0.015 [S], soft small
  wheels are worse].

| Case | F_rr N | Steady current per motor A (six motors A) | Steady speed m/s | Margin at 1 A limit | Margin at stall | Grade at 1 A | Grade at stall | a at 1 A m/s² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13.3 kg, Crr 0.02 | 2.60 | 0.385 (2.31) | 0.54 | 3.6x | 5.7x | 3.0° | 5.4° | 0.51 |
| 13.3 kg, Crr 0.04 | 5.21 | 0.620 (3.72) | 0.43 | 1.8x | 2.9x | 1.9° | 4.3° | 0.32 |
| 11.0 kg, Crr 0.02 | 2.16 | 0.345 (2.07) | 0.56 | 4.4x | 6.9x | 3.9° | 6.8° | 0.66 |
| 11.0 kg, Crr 0.04 | 4.31 | 0.540 (3.24) | 0.47 | 2.2x | 3.5x | 2.7° | 5.7° | 0.46 |

Formulas: steady per-motor torque T = F_rr r / 6; current I = 0.15 + T / k_t; speed
n = 200 (1 - T / T_stall); grade sin θ = (F_avail - F_rr) / W; acceleration
a = (F_avail - F_rr) / m.

- Speed: no-load 200 RPM at 6 V gives v = 200 x 2π/60 x 0.0315 = **0.66 m/s**. At 5 V,
  scaling speed with voltage [A]: 167 RPM, **0.55 m/s**. Under the steady rolling load at 6 V:
  0.43-0.54 m/s (table). Stall torque at 5 V by linear interpolation of the 3 V and 6 V
  values [A]: 0.667 kgf·cm; stall current between the 4.5 V (1.2 A) and 6 V (1.5 A) points
  [S]: ~1.3 A.
- Conclusion: six motors move 13.3 kg on a level hard floor. Margin is 3.6x at the 1 A limit
  on a good floor (Crr 0.02), 1.8x on a poor floor (Crr 0.04). Grade capability is 2-3° at
  the 1 A limit and 4-5° at stall. Carpet, door thresholds above ~5 mm, and ramps are outside
  the envelope. Turning, not straight driving, is the limiting case (section 3).
- Tests: (1) weigh the assembled robot; (2) pull test on the target floor with a spring scale
  at constant slow speed, expected 2.6-5.2 N; (3) measure one motor's stall current and
  no-load current on the bench to correct k_t; (4) 10 minute continuous drive, log motor case
  temperature with an IR thermometer, stop at 70 °C; (5) push the robot with the motors
  unpowered to check the gearboxes back-drive (they must, or a stalled motor drags).

## 3. Turning

Layout: differential drive on the two outer feet, castering centre foot. Track between outer
foot centrelines W_t = 0.39 m [A, scaled from the club stance]; turn radius for spin-in-place
R = 0.195 m. Load on each outer foot in the three-leg stance N_foot = 0.35 W = **45.6 N**
[A, 35 % each outer foot, 30 % centre; section 7 shows the current geometry gives less on the
centre foot]. Per axle 22.8 N, per wheel 11.4 N. Kinetic friction silicone on a hard floor
μ = 0.6 [A; sources give 0.4-0.8 kinetic on smooth metal and 0.85 static soft rubber on
concrete].

Spin-in-place about the robot centre. Each foot yaws with the body. The two axles in a foot
sit at ±L_a/2 from the foot centre, so each axle slides sideways at ω L_a/2 (velocity of a
point at (±L_a/2, R) under yaw rate ω is (-ωR, ±ωL_a/2)). Lateral scrub moment per foot:

M_scrub = 2 x (μ N_axle) x (L_a/2) = μ N_foot L_a / 2

| L_a mm | M_scrub N·m per foot |
|---|---|
| 45 | 0.615 |
| 52 | 0.711 |
| 60 | 0.820 |

Second term, solid-axle effect: the two wheels on one axle are 60 mm apart [A, 36.6 mm tip
span plus hubs] and sit at different radii from the turn centre, so their path speeds differ
by ±ω x 0.03 m against ωR = ω x 0.195 m, that is ±15 %. One wheel of each pair must slip
longitudinally. Upper bound moment per axle = μ N_w x 0.060 = 0.6 x 11.4 x 0.060 =
**0.41 N·m**, 0.82 N·m per foot. Two wheels on one axle are mechanically fine (no lateral
scrub, no bearing load), but they do add this yaw resistance when the pair saturates.

Required drive force per side F = (M_scrub + M_axle) / R + Crr N_foot, with Crr N_foot =
0.02 x 45.6 = 0.91 N:

| L_a mm | F per side, lateral scrub only N | F per side with solid-axle term N | Available per side at 1 A N | Available at stall N |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45 | 4.07 | 8.27 | 4.70 | 7.47 |
| 52 | 4.56 | 8.76 | 4.70 | 7.47 |
| 60 | 5.12 | 9.32 | 4.70 | 7.47 |

Sensitivity at L_a = 45 mm: μ 0.4 gives 3.0 N (5.8 N with axle term); μ 0.8 gives 5.1 N
(10.7 N).

Conclusion: spin-in-place is at or beyond the drive limit. With lateral scrub alone the 1 A
limit is just met at 45 mm spacing and exceeded at 52-60 mm. With the solid-axle term the
demand exceeds even stall. Arc turns reduce the scrub: the axle slip angle is
(L_a/2)/R_turn = 1.3° at R_turn = 1 m, 2.6° at 0.5 m, and the wheel-pair speed difference is
3 % at 1 m, so the tyres run below saturation [A; tyre cornering stiffness not known].
Recommendations: L_a = 45 mm minimum practical; keep the track as wide as the body allows
(the required force scales with 1/R); command arc turns with R_turn >= 0.5 m by default and
allow spin only at full 6 V with a time limit; move weight from the outer feet to the centre
foot (section 7) because M_scrub scales with N_foot. Centre caster: swivel friction on two
608 bearings is small (< 0.05 N·m [A]) and is neglected; give the caster >= 30 mm trail [A]
so it aligns within a quarter turn.

Test: fit one finished foot with two motors and four wheels, load it to 46 N on the target
floor, and measure the torque to yaw it with a spring scale at a 0.2 m arm; expected 0.6-1.5
N·m. Then measure the push force the powered foot delivers, expected 4.7 N at 1 A.

## 4. Motor shaft and wheel

- TT output shaft Ø5.40 mm, D-flat to 3.70 mm, stubs 9.30 and 8.70 mm long [S drawing]. The
  shaft passes through the plastic gearbox and is supported by the two housing walls
  (18.6 mm apart [S]).
- Load per wheel, even split: 13.27 / 12 = 1.11 kg = **10.8 N**. On an outer foot with the
  35 % share: 45.6 / 4 = **11.4 N**.
- Wheel centre plane 12 mm outboard of the housing face [A]. Bending moment at the housing
  M = 11.4 x 0.012 = 0.137 N·m. Solid-round approximation σ = 32M/(πd³) = 32 x 0.137 /
  (π x 0.0054³) = **8.8 MPa**; with a 3x dynamic factor for kerb drops [A] 26.5 MPa. A steel
  shaft is fine; the D-flat raises the local stress but stays well under yield.
- Bearing pressure on the housing bore, wall 1.5 mm [A]: 11.4 / (5.4 x 1.5) = **1.4 MPa**,
  4.2 MPa dynamic. Static allowable for POM/nylon is ~10-20 MPa [A], so wear and slop, not
  fracture, is the failure mode. If the bores wear, the radial load moves onto the output
  gear teeth and the gear skips.
- Lateral scrub (section 3) pushes each wheel along its shaft with up to μ N_w = 6.8 N. The
  3766 is a press fit [S] with no retaining screw, so wheels can walk outward.
- Recommendations: (1) keep the wheel hub within 1 mm of the gearbox face (a = 12 mm is the
  minimum the shaft geometry allows); (2) add an outboard printed journal that supports the
  wheel hub outer diameter (measure the hub on a real 3766 before modelling) or a printed
  thrust face 0.5 mm outside the hub so the wheel cannot walk off; (3) clamp the gearbox in a
  printed saddle using both Ø3.0 mm holes 17.6 mm apart [S] and the rear tab hole so the
  housing, not the motor can, carries the load; (4) design the foot so a wheel can be
  replaced without removing the motor.
- Test: run one loaded foot (46 N) for one hour of figure-8 driving; measure shaft radial play
  before and after with a dial indicator (reject > 0.3 mm growth); check wheel axial position.

## 5. Head drive

- Dome mass 0.95 kg print plus 0.2 kg of displays, LEDs and wiring = **1.15 kg**, W_d = 11.3 N
  [A].
- Lazy-susan friction: ball race at r_b = 0.12 m [A], rolling coefficient 0.02 [A]:
  T_b = 0.02 x 11.3 x 0.12 = 0.027 N·m. Slip-ring drag and dome-gap rubbing add ~0.03 N·m
  [A]. Design steady dome torque **T_dome = 0.10 N·m** with margin.
- Dome inertia as a hemispherical shell J = 2/3 m r² = 0.0193 kg·m²; 0 to 15 RPM in 0.5 s
  needs α = 3.14 rad/s², T = 0.061 N·m. Start torque total ~0.16 N·m.
- Friction wheel on the inside of the dome lip ring at R_ring = 0.145 m [A, ring ID 290 mm].
  Ratio R_ring / r_wheel = 145 / 31.5 = **4.6**.
- Tangential force F_t = 0.10 / 0.145 = 0.69 N (1.1 N at start). With μ = 0.8 silicone on
  PETG [A, given] the normal force needed is 0.86 N steady, **1.4 N at start**. Recommend a
  spring preload of **3 to 5 N** (2-3x), set by the tension screw; this adds only 0.06 N·m of
  bending on the motor shaft.
- Wheel torque T_w = 0.10 / 4.6 = 0.0217 N·m = 28 % of 6 V stall, current 0.15 + 0.0217 /
  0.0581 = **0.52 A**.
- Dome speed at 200 motor RPM = 200 / 4.6 = **43 RPM**, far too fast (a screen-accurate dome
  turns ~10-20 RPM [A]) and the slip ring is rated to 300 RPM [S] so it is not the limit.
- Recommended PWM: **50 % at 6 V** (3 V effective). At 3 V the stall torque is 0.4 kgf·cm
  = 0.039 N·m [S], margin 1.8x over T_w; loaded speed 90 x (1 - 0.0217 / 0.039) = 40 RPM,
  **dome 8.7 RPM**, 20 RPM at no load. Use 35-60 % PWM as the speed range and a 0.5 s ramp.
  Below 35 % the motor stalls under the assumed load.
- Test: with the dome on its bearing, pull it round with a spring scale at the rim; expected
  0.7 N steady at 0.158 m (0.1 N·m). If it exceeds 1.5 N, raise PWM to 60 % or lighten the
  dome before changing the wheel.

## 6. Battery and runtime

5 V rail idle [A unless marked]: Pi 4 0.60 A (measured idle 0.575 A [S]; up to 0.9 A with
Wi-Fi streaming and audio), two LED matrices 0.15 A, NeoPixels average 0.30 A (30 pixels at
10 mA average), TFT 0.10 A, KB2040 0.05 A, audio amp 0.20 A average. Total **1.40 A at 5 V =
7.0 W**. At 90 % converter efficiency: 7.8 W = **0.65 A from 12 V**.

Driving [A from section 2]: six motors at 0.385 A x 6 V = 13.9 W plus head motor 0.5 A x 3 V
= 1.5 W: 15.4 W / 0.9 = **1.43 A from 12 V**. Total while driving **2.07 A**. Peak, seven
motors at the 1 A limit: 42 W / 0.9 = 3.9 A, plus idle **4.5 A from 12 V**. Stall transients
above the limit last milliseconds because the DRV8833 chops at 1 A [S].

Usable capacity: 7 Ah at the 20 h rate but 6.7 Ah at the 10 h rate [S], so 6.7 Ah applies at
1-2 A. At 50 % depth of discharge: **3.35 Ah**.

| Profile | 12 V current A | Runtime h |
|---|---|---|
| Idle, displays on | 0.65 | 5.2 |
| Continuous driving | 2.07 | 1.6 |
| Mixed, 50 % driving | 1.36 | 2.5 |

Recharge with the 1 A charger [S]: 3.5 Ah / 1 A / 0.85 charge efficiency [A] = 4.1 h bulk,
then absorption to the float point, **about 6 h total**. The battery allows 2.16 A maximum
charge [S], so a 1 A charger is within its limit. Battery short-circuit current is roughly
12.8 V / 0.028 Ω = **457 A** [A from the S internal resistance]; the datasheet rates 108 A for
5 s [S]. A fuse at the battery terminal is mandatory.

| Pi converter input (12 V to 5 V 3 A, 15 W) | 1.4 A | 2 A |
| Dome feed through slip ring | 1.2 A | 1.5 A (polyfuse at 5 V) |

Wire gauge (Powerstream chassis ratings [S]): main battery leads and fuse block 16 AWG
(22 A); motor converter input and 6 V bus 18 AWG (16 A); 5 V Pi feed 18 AWG kept under 0.25 m
so the loop drop at 3 A is 31 mV (20 AWG: 50 mV; Pi needs > 4.75 V); per-motor extensions
22 AWG (7 A); the stock 28 AWG motor leads (1.4 A chassis rating [S]) are acceptable at the
1 A limit but drop 85 mV over the 0.2 m pair, so shorten them where possible; charge port
20 AWG; slip ring 2 A per wire [S], use two wires each for 5 V and ground in the dome.

## 7. Structure

Geometry for the three-leg stance [A throughout]: outer legs vertical, body tilted 18°,
shoulder axis directly above the outer foot centres, centre foot contact 0.29 m ahead of the
outer foot line, foot contact patches ±0.026 m about each foot centre, centre of gravity
height 0.35 m, shoulder axis 0.27 m up the body axis, body-group CG 0.09 m below the shoulder
axis on the body axis, dome-group CG 0.35 m above it.

### Centre of gravity and foot loads

Horizontal offsets from the outer-foot line (forward positive): dome group 1.4 kg at
-0.35 sin 18° = -0.108 m; body, battery, electronics 6.9 kg at +0.09 sin 18° = +0.028 m;
outer legs 2.46 kg and outer feet 1.37 kg at 0; centre leg and foot 1.2 kg at +0.29 m.
x_cg = (1.4 x -0.108 + 6.9 x 0.028 + 1.2 x 0.29) / 13.3 = **+0.029 m**. Centre-foot share =
0.029 / 0.29 = **10 %** (13 N); each outer foot 58.5 N. This is too rear-heavy. Target: x_cg
>= 0.08 m, centre foot >= 28 % [A]. Moving the 2.26 kg battery 0.10 m forward in the body
moves the CG 12 mm; the rest must come from putting the shoulder axis lower in the body or
raking the legs forward. Recalculate when the CAD fixes the shoulder height.

### Tipping

- Tip-back (accelerating forward, or climbing facing uphill), current geometry: a_tip =
  g (x_cg + 0.026) / h = 9.81 x 0.055 / 0.35 = **1.55 m/s²**, static tilt 9.0°. The drive
  can deliver 0.5-0.9 m/s², so driving alone does not tip it, but a 20 mm bump at speed can.
  At the target x_cg = 0.08 m: 2.97 m/s², 16.8°.
- Tip-forward (braking): a = 9.81 x (0.29 + 0.026 - 0.029) / 0.35 = 8.0 m/s². Not a concern.
- Lateral: a = 9.81 x 0.195 / 0.35 = 5.5 m/s², static tilt 29°. Not a concern.
- Test: tilt board, measure the tip-back angle (accept >= 15°); bathroom scale under the
  centre foot (accept >= 28 % of total mass).

### Shoulder joint (M12 bolt, 30 mm printed boss)

With the legs vertical and the joint locked by the index pin, the static shoulder moment is
near zero: the body-group moment about the shoulder axis (93.2 N x 0.041 m = 3.8 N·m) is
balanced by the centre-foot reaction (13.1 N x 0.29 m = 3.8 N·m). Design case is the robot
rocked back onto the rear axle contacts with the centre foot lifted: M = 93.2 x (0.041 +
0.026) = 6.2 N·m, times a 3x dynamic factor [A] = 18.7 N·m total, **9.4 N·m per shoulder**.
Vertical shear per shoulder V = 58.5 x 3 = **176 N**.

- Bolt shear τ = 176 / (π/4 x 12²) = 1.6 MPa; M12 class 8.8 proof load 580 x 84.3 =
  48.9 kN [S]. Steel is never the limit here.
- Bearing on the printed boss σ = V / (d t) = 176 / (12 x 30) = **0.49 MPa**. Allowable for
  PETG at one quarter of the 34-47 MPa tensile strength [S] is ~8 MPa: 16x margin.
- Index pin (M6 steel at 45 mm radius [A]) carries the moment: F = 9.4 / 0.045 = 208 N;
  bearing 208 / (6 x 30) = 1.2 MPa: 7x margin. Use two pins or a serrated face to avoid
  relying on one pin.
- Preload: do not clamp printed faces with a torqued M12. Fit a steel crush sleeve through the
  boss so the bolt clamps steel to steel, and use 40 mm steel washers: at 2.1 kN preload
  (5 N·m torque, T = 0.2 F d) the face pressure is 2.1 kN / 1124 mm² = 1.9 MPa, low enough
  to avoid PETG creep at room temperature [A]; a 24 mm washer gives 6.6 MPa, which creeps.
  PETG softens at 62-68 °C [S], so keep the robot out of a hot car.

### Body ring hoop strength

Shoulder loads enter the shell as vertical in-plane shear: 176 / (4 mm x 100 mm boss height)
= 0.35 MPa. Worst radial case, two opposite radial loads P = 141 N (47 N x 3) on a thin ring
of radius 158.5 mm: M = 0.318 P R = 7,107 N·mm. Over a 100 mm effective shell height the
section modulus Z = 100 x 4² / 6 = 267 mm³ and σ = 26.7 MPa (marginal against 34 MPa Bambu
XY); over the full 385 mm ring with the ring ribs Z = 1,027 mm³ and σ = 6.9 MPa. The ring
ribs are what makes the shell work; keep at least one ring rib within 30 mm of each shoulder
boss. Stiffness, not strength, governs; a 3 mm wall with the same ribs is acceptable and saves
0.4 kg (section 1).

### Leg bending (two M8 rods plus PETG box)

Design load: lateral scrub or a side push at the foot, F = μ N = 0.6 x 58.5 = **35 N**, at
0.43 m: M = 15.1 N·m at the shoulder, x1.5 = 22.7 N·m, use **20 N·m**.

- One M8 rod, minor diameter 6.647 mm: I = π d⁴ / 64 = 95.8 mm⁴, A = 34.7 mm². Two rods
  20 mm apart in the bending plane with composite action: I = 2 (95.8 + 34.7 x 10²) =
  7,130 mm⁴, EI = 200,000 x 7,130 = 1.43 x 10⁹ N·mm². Without composite action: 3.8 x 10⁷.
- PETG box 80 x 50 x 4, bending about the 80 mm axis: I = (80 x 50³ - 72 x 42³) / 12 =
  388,805 mm⁴, E = 1,600 MPa [S range 1.5-1.8 GPa], EI = 6.2 x 10⁸ N·mm².
- Combined EI 2.0 x 10⁹ (composite) or 6.6 x 10⁸ (rods loose). PETG share of the moment 30 %
  or 94 %; PETG stress σ = M c / I = 0.4 or 1.2 MPa. Tip deflection at 35 N:
  δ = F L³ / 3EI = 0.46 or 1.4 mm. Column buckling of the box at 430 mm: 33 kN. The beam is
  not the risk.
- The risk is the print-to-print joint at mid-leg: M there = 35 x 0.215 = 7.6 N·m across a
  layer-line face with 18-23 MPa interlayer strength [S]. Preload each M8 rod to 1 kN (1.6
  N·m torque, 16 mm washers, 5 MPa under the washer): two rods at 1 kN with a 25 mm lever to
  the face edge hold the joint closed to 50 N·m, 6.6x the demand. M8 class 4.6 proof load is
  8.2 kN [S], so 1 kN is 12 % of proof.
- Shoulder hub to leg body: the hub is a separate thick print; carry the M8 rods through it
  and into the body-side boss so the load path is steel end to end.

### Centre caster bearing

Load target 30 % of W = 39 N, x3 dynamic = **117 N** axial on the pivot. A 608-2RS is rated
1,383 N static and 3,296 N dynamic [S]; the deep-groove axial allowance of ~0.25 C0 [A] is
345 N, 3x margin. Use two 608s 20 mm apart on an M8 pivot: the caster couple (40 mm trail x
23 N lateral = 0.94 N·m) gives 47 N radial per bearing, negligible. Seat the bearings in a
printed bore with a steel spacer between inner races so the M8 preload does not squeeze the
outer races.

## 8. Ranked risks and the test that retires each

1. **Turning scrub exceeds drive force.** Spin-in-place needs 4.1-9.3 N per side against
   4.7 N available at the 1 A limit. Test: one loaded foot (46 N) on the target floor, measure
   yaw torque with a spring scale at a 0.2 m arm (expect 0.6-1.5 N·m), then measure the
   powered push force (expect 4.7 N at 1 A). Retire if a 0.5 m arc at 50 % PWM completes
   without a stall.
2. **Mass over the 11 kg target.** Every extra kilogram cuts the drive margin and runtime and
   raises scrub. Test: weigh every print on a kitchen scale as it comes off the bed, keep a
   running total in `bom/`, stop and lighten when the running total passes the group budget in
   section 1.
3. **TT gearbox shaft bores and press-fit wheels under 11-14 N radial plus 7 N lateral.**
   Test: one hour figure-8 on a loaded foot, dial-indicator shaft play before and after
   (reject > 0.3 mm), wheel axial position unchanged.
4. **Tip-back with the CG 29 mm ahead of the outer-foot line.** Test: tilt board to >= 15°
   backward without lifting the centre foot; bathroom scale under the centre foot reads
   >= 28 % of total mass. If not, move the battery forward and lower the shoulder axis.
5. **Motor heating and runtime at the 1 A limit.** Test: 20 minutes of continuous driving on
   the worst floor in the house; motor case temperature < 70 °C by IR thermometer, battery
   above 11.5 V after one hour of mixed use, no DRV8833 thermal shutdown.

Lower-ranked: head friction-wheel slip (retired by the rim pull test in section 5); PETG creep
under the shoulder bolt (retired by the crush-sleeve design and a 48 h loaded soak at 40 °C).
